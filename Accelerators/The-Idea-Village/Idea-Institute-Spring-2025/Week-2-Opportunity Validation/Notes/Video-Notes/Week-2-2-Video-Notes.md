# ❌ Mistakes
---

1. Death By PowerPoint
2. Death By Demo

### Summary of Customer Interview Mistakes
- Many great entrepreneurs show the slide deck or demos to customers thinking that they have created all the features that the customer will want because they think they understand the customer. 
    - Because of this they think potential customers will pay.
    - This is a major mistake. 
    - They make a set of assumptions or hypotheses are no more than guesses.
    - You need to listen and see if your assumptions match to what the customers believe.


# 🤔 Understanding the Problem
---

So, in the first interviews, you are trying to understand three things:
1. First, how does the customer do their job? Assuming you are not a domain expert.
2. Secondly, the Customer Archetype. You don't have 20 different customers, typically they fall into buckets.
3. If your assumption about the problem and the value of the problem is shared by the customer.


# 🧪 Testing the Solution
---

- Reframe from demoing your product. You want to extract information from the customer.
- You can describe what features you will have, but do not show anything.
- Allow the customer to tell you their pain points.


# 📊 Data vs Insights
---

- Customer Discovery is about collecting a bunch of customer data so you can make decisions by extracting insights on the data.
- By only talking to a few people does not allow you to understand the customer problem, the customer archetype, how they spend the day, etc.
- Recommended to talk to 10 - 15 customers a week via online video tools such as Skype, Zoom, Google Video, etc. or in person.
- Survey tools do not work here; you need open ended questions and to build upon customer insights.


# 🧠 Truly Understanding Customers
---

## Finding out Who Your Customer is
You want to gather data and insights on: 
- Who they are?
- What their problems are?
- How are they solving the problems today?
- What their alternatives are?
- What they pay for?
- Whether or not your product is valuable to them?
