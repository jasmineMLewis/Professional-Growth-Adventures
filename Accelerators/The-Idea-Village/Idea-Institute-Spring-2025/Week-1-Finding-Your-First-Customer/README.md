# 🧠 Weekly Learning Theme
## 📚 Session Overview (Written Context on LMS)
> Welcome to week 1 of IDEAinstitute! Everyone in this program is at a different stage in their venture's development and the businesses (and social enterprises) you are working on are very different. The goal here is to move forward and make progress as you can given where you are now. Whatever stage you are in, it's exciting to see enthusiasm build up and to see smiling faces eager to engage. I hope to see that energy in the next session.

This week we will turn our attention to your first customer. Who will be the first customer to want your product or service? How broad or narrow should that segment be? The goal here is to turn a fuzzy mental image of a potential customer you might have now into a useful persona you can leverage to design a marketing campaign or visualize as you are building your prototype. The better your customer persona, the easier it will be to hypothesize all aspects of your business - so give it all you've got!

Below, you can find the video that corresponds with this chapter in Entrepreneurship in the Wild. You will also find an additional reading and a worksheet where you can begin mapping your customer personas. Fill out that pdf and share it in the FEED section of the MORROW platform.
