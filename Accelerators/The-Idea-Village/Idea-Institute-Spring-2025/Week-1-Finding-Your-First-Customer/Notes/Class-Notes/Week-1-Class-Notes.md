# 🧑‍💼 Customer Centric
## General
- Focus on a specific niche
- You want word of mouth
- The more specific you can be about your customer, then you can acquire a customer base

## Build a Profile/Persona
- When you create a Profile of who your customer might be, you can find the problems customers have
- It helps find who your customer is and not your customer
- Put yourself in their customer shoes.

## Who do We Prioritize?
- Start from the Customer
- In their mind, determine how they solve the problem
- What is their top problem.
- Focus on their top problem. So, you don't have to have to find. How do the customer rank the problem.
- As an entrepreneur, find how you can solve their problem

## Define Customer
- Line up the Personas
- Rank them by their willingness to pay, is their searching for them actively, is it something they will pay long-term

## How to determine Customer?
- When you build a two-sided market, it complicates it.
- Try to understand the role and who are making the decisions.
- Understand the market of supply and demand.
- So look for the Market Balance in two sided markets.
- Sometimes it's not a monolithic customer. It's broken down into people who actually make the decisions.
- Don't start interviewing without trying to define your Persona.
- Try to find a real person and build a Persona around them.
- Are the individual you interviewing talking about the problem in the same way I am?
